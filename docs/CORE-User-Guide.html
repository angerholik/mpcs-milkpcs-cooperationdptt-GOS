CORE

Cooperative Oversight & Reporting Engine — Quick Guide for Inspectors
Department of Cooperation
Government of Sikkim

Who uses what

  • Field app (this guide) — used by CI, ACI and PA. You only see the institutions assigned to you (a CI sees the ones they registered).
  • CORE ADMIN — used by the CI (and the System Admin) to review reports and assign institutions to ACI / PA. ACI and PA cannot open it. See the separate CI Guide.

1. Open the app

https://core-cooperationdptt-gos.vercel.app
  • Open the address in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
  • Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app: Android — menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. iPhone — Share → Add to Home Screen.

2. Log in

  • Use your official email ID and password, then tap SIGN IN.
  • Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password?, then open the link sent to your email. Check Spam if it does not arrive in a few minutes.
  • New inspector? Tap Register here. You can only work on institutions your CI has assigned to you, so ask your CI to assign them after you register.

3. Monthly report — step by step

  1. Choose your society or centre and the reporting month.
  2. Fill each section. Tap Save and continue to move to the next one; your data is saved as you go.
  3. MPCS sections: Evidence, Sales, Business, Loan (only if the society has an active loan), Activities. Milk PCS follows its own section list.
  4. Evidence: take the photo on site. The photo, time and location are printed on the report.
  5. Activities: pick a category (Meetings, Trainings, Events, Others), enter the number of participants and the summary / resolution, then tap Add to log. Add one entry per activity.
  6. Go to Review and submit only when every section is done. Check the figures, then submit.

4. Other things you can do

  • Members: add, edit and number the society's members. Tap a member to edit.
  • CSC transactions: add one entry per transaction — service (for example phone recharge, flight or train ticket), the total amount and the commission.
  • Records: see past reports and open their PDF.
  • Print / Save PDF: opens the report as a PDF you can save or share.
Important: each submitted report's PDF is kept for 7 days. Download or save it within that time. The record itself stays in the system.

5. If something goes wrong

  • Refresh the page (pull down) and try again. Make sure you have a mobile signal.
  • Do not submit the same month twice. If a report needs correcting, contact the person below.
  • Take a screenshot of any error message and send it with your name, society and month.
