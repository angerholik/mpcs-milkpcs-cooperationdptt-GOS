1Open the app

core-cooperationdptt-gos.vercel.app
  • Open it in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
  • Add to Home screen so it opens like an app: Android — menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen; iPhone — Share → Add to Home Screen.

2Log in

  • Enter your official email ID and password, then tap SIGN IN.
  • Forgot password? Tap it and open the link we email you. Check Spam if it is slow.
  • New inspector? Tap Register here. Then ask your CI to assign your institutions. Until then you will see none.

3File the monthly report

  1. Pick your society or centre and the reporting month.
  2. Fill each section and tap Save and continue. Your data saves as you go.
  3. MPCS: Evidence → Sales → Business → Loan (only if the society has an active loan) → Activities. Milk PCS has its own list of sections.
  4. Evidence: take the photo on site. Photo, time and location print on the report.
  5. Review and submit only when every section is done. Check the figures, then submit.

Activities log

  • Choose a category: Meetings, Trainings, Events or Others.
  • Enter No. of Participants and the Summary / resolution, then tap Add to log.
  • One entry per activity. Each is printed on the report.

CSC transactions & Members

  • CSC: one entry per transaction — service (phone recharge, flight or train ticket and more), total amount and commission.
  • Members: add and edit the society's members. They are numbered automatically.

Reports & PDF

  • Records lists past reports. Open one to see its PDF.
  • Print / Save PDF lets you save or share the report.
Keep your PDF: each submitted report's PDF is kept for 7 days. Save it in that time. The record itself stays in the system.

If something goes wrong

  • Pull down to refresh and check your mobile signal.
  • Do not submit the same month twice. To correct a report, contact support.
  • Send a screenshot of the error with your name, society and month.
