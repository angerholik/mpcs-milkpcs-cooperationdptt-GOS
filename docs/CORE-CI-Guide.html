  • You see only your own institutions and your own ACI / PA. You cannot see or change another CI's.
  • One institution can be given to more than one ACI or PA. Each one only sees what is assigned to them.

Step 1 — Register your institution (field app)

  1. Open https://core-cooperationdptt-gos.vercel.app and sign in.
  2. Open My Institutions (menu → + Add / Manage My Institutions) and tap the Add button.
  3. Choose MPCS Society or Milk PCS Unit, enter the name, registration number and address, and save.
  4. It now appears in your list and is yours to assign.

Step 2 — ACI / PA register themselves

  • Every ACI and PA registers in the field app (Register here). They appear in your Users & Roles list straight away. No Admin approval is needed, and you do not create accounts.
  • Until you assign them an institution, they can sign in but see no institutions.

Who sees whom

  • You (CI): yourself, your own ACI / PA, and every ACI / PA that no CI has taken yet. You never see another CI.
  • Once a CI assigns an ACI / PA to an institution, they belong to that CI and disappear from every other CI's list.
  • If a CI revokes all of an officer's institutions, that officer returns to the shared pool for all CIs.
  • System Admin: sees everyone and which CI assigned which ACI / PA (Governance Tree).

Step 3 — Assign an institution (CORE ADMIN)

https://admin-ten-rho-87.vercel.app
  1. Sign in with your usual CI email and password.
  2. Open Users & Roles.
  3. Tap Assign ACI to Unit or Assign PA to Unit.
  4. Select the MPCS / Milk unit, then select the officer.
  5. Confirm. A message says the officer is now assigned to that unit.

Shortcut: in the officer's row, tap ⋮ → Assign institution (it names the officer for you). On a phone, use Assign another institution on the officer's card.

Change or remove an assignment

  • Reassign Unit — hands that institution to a different ACI / PA.
  • Revoke — removes their access to it straight away. You can assign it again later.
  • View Governance Tree — shows who has which institution.
Check it worked: ask the ACI / PA to open the field app → View My Institutions. The institution should be listed, and they can now file its monthly report. If the list is empty, ask them to sign out and sign in again.
